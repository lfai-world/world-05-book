# 05 · Actions on events — signals between drawers

When a conversation lands in the inbox, or the owner approves at the gate, a small workflow sends one signal to the private runner. The runner wakes on a clean cloud machine, runs all twelve stations, and refreshes this dashboard and the profile's numbers. See the [Automation table](https://lfai-world.github.io/world-05-book/factory/#automation).

The signal needs a key; it lives only in the private repositories' Actions secrets.

## Say this
> "새 대화 들어오면 공장 한 바퀴 자동으로 돌게 해."
*(Run the line automatically whenever a new conversation arrives.)*
