# 10 · Proof — numbers that update themselves

The badges on [the profile](https://github.com/lfai-world) read small JSON files in [`badges/`](https://github.com/lfai-world/world-05-book/tree/main/badges) that the runner rewrites after every run; the profile's *Live* table is rewritten the same way. Nobody edits the numbers by hand, so they can be trusted — and when the line stops, the *updated* badge shows it.

## Say this
> "숫자는 손으로 쓰지 마. 공장이 돌 때마다 스스로 바꾸게 해."
*(Don't write numbers by hand — let the factory update them on every run.)*
